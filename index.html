Disclaimer: This calculator gives an estimate for illustration only and is not tax, financial or legal advice. It uses the standard Personal Allowance and the Income Tax rates for England, Wales and Northern Ireland, and employee (Class 1, category A) National Insurance. Savings interest uses the Personal Savings Allowance for the selected tax year, and any interest above it is taxed at the basic or higher rate depending on which band your taxable income falls in; the starting rate for savings and interest that spans two bands are not included. Pension contributions are assumed to come out of your pay before Income Tax but not before National Insurance; relief at source and salary sacrifice arrangements are not included. It does not include Scottish rates, student loan repayments, benefits in kind, other income or any tax code adjustments. Your actual pay may differ. Please check the current rates on GOV.UK or speak to a qualified adviser.
